The meaning

Give a feeling
a form.

Stop This Planet gives form to a familiar feeling: it is possible to love life and still be deeply disappointed by the way people sometimes treat one another. Its manifesto is an objection to cruelty, humiliation and needless suffering.

The mobile artwork turns that idea into a fictional departure experience: a personal digital ticket, a passenger number and signature, an avatar, and a quiet capsule in space.

The manifesto is free to read. The ticket is a created digital object that supports the independent project and its author.

The application

In development.

The app is currently in internal testing. Public ticket sales have not opened. This website presents the manifesto and project information; it does not take payments.
